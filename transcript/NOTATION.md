# Transcript notation (v3.4)

Lines 1–20 are page 1, lines 21–25 page 2; the last line, `eimat`, sits below the squares and is followed on the paper by ten dots (nine on the line, the last raised).

| Written | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `a`–`z` | letters as written. The writer's *r* looks like a small *z* sitting on the line; it is written `r`. A true *z* has a tail below the line and is written `z`. |
| `'` after a letter | a small comma-like mark: **the last letter of a word** in the song |
| `"` | two ticks (once, line 21) |
| `ê` | *e* with a hat: **a word-final e** |
| `ö`, `ü` | as written (the song's umlauts) |
| `f2` | an *f* with a small raised loop (line 13) |
| `x.y.z.` | a dotted group: letters with dots between them, placed in reserved cells |
| `.` at the end of a word | full stop; the last letter of each square is also underlined on the paper |
| `A` | the only capital letter (line 22) |

Squares end at the underlined letters: after `nenet'se.` (line 5), `rf'uaf'np.` (9), `ngwsaetme.` (13, double underline), `eamdemn.` (17), `n'aêain.` (22) and `wrireheu.` (25).

Sources: the 2015 newspaper photographs and a 2015 forum transcription (d3.ru), Thomas Ernst's 2017 transcription, press scans from the *Strana Kaliningrad* print edition, and a symbol-by-symbol re-reading in 2026. Page 2 is water-damaged; a few of its letters remain uncertain.
