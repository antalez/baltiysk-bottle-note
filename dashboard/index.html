Baltiysk Bottle Note

The note found in a bottle in Baltiysk in 2015 is the GDR song "Einheitslied" (Freundschaftskantate der Jugend; words Herbert Keller, music André Asriel), cut into six consecutive pieces. Each piece was written into a 13 × 13 square with its letters jumbled. Here every square is matched against its piece of the song: the letters the text uses are crossed out, and what remains is shown.
